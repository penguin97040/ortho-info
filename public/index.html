A plain-English guide

Orthodoxy, explained for Protestants.

Icons, incense, chanting, and standing for an hour and a half. If you grew up Protestant, your first Orthodox service can feel like another world. These short pages explain what’s going on, what Orthodox Christians actually believe, and how people join.

Start with the basics → Common misconceptions

Going for the first time?

  • Stand near the back and watch. Nobody expects you to know what to do.

  • Sit when you need to. Standing is traditional, but no one minds.

  • Don’t go up for Communion. It’s for Orthodox Christians. The blessed bread at the end is usually fine.

  • Stay for coffee if there is any. That’s where the questions get answered.
