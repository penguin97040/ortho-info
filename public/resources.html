Keep learning

In short This site is only a starting point. These are the books and resources people most often recommend next, plus a glossary for the jargon.

Books

  • Welcome to the Orthodox Church · Frederica Mathewes-Green

    A warm, easy tour from someone who converted as an adult. A good first read.

  • The Orthodox Way · Kallistos Ware

    Short and thoughtful: what Orthodox believe about God, prayer and the spiritual life.

  • The Orthodox Church · Timothy (Kallistos) Ware

    The classic overview of history and belief. More of a reference book.

  • Thirsting for God in a Land of Shallow Wells · Matthew Gallatin

    A former evangelical pastor explains what changed his mind. Very relevant if you grew up Protestant.

  • For the Life of the World · Alexander Schmemann

    A short classic on why worship and the sacraments matter to everyday life.

Online

  • Ancient Faith: podcasts and talks on every topic, including plenty for enquirers.
  • The Orthodox Faith: a clear online guide to doctrine and worship.
  • Serbian Orthodox Church: the official site (mostly in Serbian).

Glossary

Antidoron
Blessed bread given out at the end of the Liturgy. Serbs call it nafora.
Catechumen
Someone formally preparing to join the Church.
Chrismation
Anointing with holy oil, the gift of the Holy Spirit. It’s how most people are received.
Divine Liturgy
The main Sunday service, with Communion.
Icon
A sacred image of Christ, Mary or a saint.
Iconostasis
The wall of icons in front of the altar.
Jesus Prayer
“Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, have mercy on me, a sinner.”
Kum / kuma
Godparent or sponsor (Serbian).
Mysteries
The Orthodox word for sacraments, such as Communion, baptism and confession.
Pascha
Easter. From the Hebrew word for Passover.
Slava
A Serbian family’s yearly feast for its patron saint.
Theosis
Growing into union with God by his grace. The goal of the Christian life.
Theotokos
“God-bearer”: Mary, the Mother of God.
Vespers
The evening service, often on Saturday night.
